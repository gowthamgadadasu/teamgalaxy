The Three-Tier Approval Lifecycle

Every permission follows an accountable, institutional sequence.

1

Student Submits Request

Students initiate the request with event brochures, date ranges, and technical contest documentation.

2

Proctor Verification

The designated proctor evaluates student eligibility, verifies attendance criteria, and appends official recommendation.

3

In-Charge Final Decision

The Technical Event In-Charge grants formal authorization and generates a verifiable clearance pass.
